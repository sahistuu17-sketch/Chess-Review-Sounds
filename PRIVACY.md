# Privacy Policy – Chess Review Sounds

_Last updated: 2026-11-23_

Chess Review Sounds does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data.

**What the extension stores.** The sounds you choose, your trim settings, the volume and the
on/off switches are saved in your browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`).
This data stays on your device. It is never sent to the developer or to any third party.

**What the extension reads.** On chess.com analysis and game review pages it reads the
classification of the selected move (for example "Brilliant" or "Blunder") from the page, only to
decide which of your sounds to play. It does not read, store or send your games, account
details, chat messages or any other page content.

**Network.** The extension makes no network requests of its own. It has no analytics, no
tracking and no remote code.

**Permissions.**
- `storage` and `unlimitedStorage`: save your chosen audio files and settings locally. Audio files can be larger than the default storage quota.
- Access to `chess.com`: run the script that detects the move classification on review pages.

**Deleting your data.** Use "Reset all" in the extension popup, or remove the extension, and all
stored data is deleted.

**Contact.** Open an issue at https://github.com/sahistuu17-sketch/Chess-Review-Sounds/issues
